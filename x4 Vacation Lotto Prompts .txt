Vacation weeks are Monday-through-Friday workweeks and are identified by their Monday start date.


For target year Y:

- Continue every seven days.
- Include the Monday on or before December 31 of year Y.
- Each generated record represents that Monday-through-Friday vacation workweek.


This intentionally allows the first generated week to begin in the previous calendar year when January 1 falls after Monday, and it allows the final workweek to extend into the next year.


The purpose is to cover every Monday-through-Friday workweek that contains at least one weekday in the selected lottery year. Depending on the calendar, this can generate 52 or 53 week records.

4. Vacation-week and weekend warning meaning


Vacation selections represent Monday through Friday.


The usual nine-day period away from work is:


- Preceding Saturday
- Preceding Sunday
- Monday through Friday vacation
- Following Saturday
- Following Sunday


Therefore, yes, the weekend warning should trigger if that same participant attempts to select First Call for any of these four dates:


- Saturday immediately before the vacation week.
- Sunday immediately before the vacation week.
- Saturday immediately after the vacation week.
- Sunday immediately after the vacation week.

The endpoint participant must never appear twice in the Active window simultaneously.


Treat each directional window at the reversal as a complete unit rather than continuously mixing the old and new directions.

A giver may choose several assignments in one offer-submission screen, but each assignment becomes a separate individual item in the common transfer pool.

- Each remains assigned to the original giver until individually accepted.
- An accepted item is removed from the pool.
- Unaccepted items remain with the original giver when the transfer round ends.
